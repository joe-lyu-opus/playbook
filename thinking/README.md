# Thinking

[中文](README.zh.md) · [Home](../README.md)

These essays explore how the [principles](../principles.md) apply under particular
conditions. They are not additional standing rules. A proposal here can be
interesting without being ready to adopt.

| Essay | Status and question |
|---|---|
| [Working with agents](ai-collaboration.md) | Practice: what context, autonomy, and verification help an agent finish useful work? |
| [Teams working with agents](ai-native-teams.md) | Synthesis: what must change around the tools for a team to benefit? |
| [Platform engineering](platform-engineering.md) | Conditional practice: how do ownership, state, lifecycles, and adoption shape a platform? |
| [Working defaults](working-defaults.md) | Local choices: which workflow and tool conventions do I use, and when would I replace them? |
| [Autonomous delivery](autonomous-delivery.md) | Proposal and thought experiment: what might sustained autonomous delivery require? Research and prices are dated, not live guidance. |

The distinction is about confidence and scope, not importance. A durable idea
can move into the principles after its reasoning holds across contexts; an
obsolete practice should be revised or retired, not promoted by repetition.
